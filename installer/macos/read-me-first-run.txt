MarkDownIt for macOS: the first launch
======================================

This build is signed ad hoc and it is not notarized, so macOS asks once
before the app is allowed to open. There are two ways past that first
prompt:

 1. Double-click the app. When the warning comes up, dismiss it, then
    open System Settings, go to Privacy & Security, and click
    "Open Anyway" on the MarkDownIt entry. On macOS 14 and older,
    right-clicking the app and choosing Open does the same in one
    step.

 2. In Terminal, clear the download flag from the app:

        xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /path/to/MarkDownIt.app

If macOS says the app is damaged, use way 2. The file is fine; the
download flag is what upsets the launch check, and clearing it is all
that is needed. The answer sticks, so later launches are plain
double-clicks.

The app needs macOS 12 or later on an Apple silicon Mac.
